FAQ

Questions we often get.

What size of company do you work with?

Mostly mid-sized companies and enterprise teams that run serious workloads but don't have a large cloud team in-house. We also build software and websites for startups.

Do you only work with Azure?

Azure is where we have the deepest experience, including large migrations and landing zones. We also work on AWS and Google Cloud, and with Kubernetes on all three.

Where is your team, and which time zones do you cover?

Our headquarters is in Chennai, India, and our specialist engineers are spread across Europe, the US and Asia-Pacific. We schedule work and meetings in your business hours.

What happens after the migration?

You choose. We hand over with full documentation and runbooks, or we stay on to run the platform as a managed service, as we did on our 600+ workload migration.

How do you price projects?

Assessments and starter packages are fixed scope and fixed price. Larger programmes are priced per phase, and managed operations as a monthly fee. You get a quote after a short call about your environment.

Can you work under an NDA and inside our own tenant?

Yes. We sign NDAs as standard and work inside your cloud tenant, under your access policies, so you keep full ownership of everything we build.
