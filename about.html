About MyCloudSME

An engineering-led cloud and software company. Headquartered in Chennai, working with clients worldwide.

Who we are

Engineers who design, build and stay to run it.

MyCloudSME Technologies Private Limited is a cloud and software engineering company. We help organisations move to the cloud, modernise the applications that hold them back, secure their environments and keep costs under control.

We work as a core team in Chennai together with a network of experienced specialist engineers in Germany, the United States, the Philippines and Australia. For each engagement we bring together the people with the right skills, working in your time zone.

Our work covers the whole lifecycle: strategy, architecture, migration, software delivery and day-to-day operations. That way what we design is also what works in production.
